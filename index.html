About the studio

A small studio, a collaborative process.

We are a small architectural design firm located in Sonoma County, California. We work collaboratively with homeowners, builders and businesses to create innovative, non-generic buildings and places that respect the local built traditions they belong to.

From farmhouse gables to Petaluma's historic streets, every project starts with the place it sits in.

Process

Four steps, one conversation.

You work with the same small team from the first site visit to the permit counter.

  1. 01

    Listen

    A site visit and a conversation about your goals, budget and timeline.

  2. 02

    Design

    Concept sketches and plans, refined together with you.

  3. 03

    Draft

    Detailed construction and permit drawings in AutoCAD.

  4. 04

    Permit & Build

    Submittal support and coordination with your builder.
